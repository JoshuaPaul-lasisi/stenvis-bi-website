FAQ

Questions we always get

How long does a typical BI project take?
+
Most dashboard and reporting projects take 3–6 weeks from discovery to delivery. Market research and feasibility studies typically run 4–6 weeks. Training workshops can be delivered in as little as 2 days. We always align on timelines during the discovery call before any work begins.
My data is a mess — scattered across Excel files, WhatsApp exports, and our POS system. Can you still help?
+
Absolutely — this is one of the most common situations we encounter. Data cleaning and consolidation is a core part of our process. We've worked with businesses running on paper records, mixed spreadsheets, and disconnected systems. The messier the starting point, the more valuable the structured output we deliver.
Do we need expensive software to use your dashboards?
+
No. We design solutions around tools you already have or can afford. Many of our clients use free or low-cost versions of Power BI, Google Looker Studio, or Excel. We always scope the tool stack to your budget before starting.
What happens after the project is delivered?
+
Every delivery includes documentation and a live walkthrough. We also offer 30-day post-launch support as standard. For ongoing needs, we offer quarterly review retainers and ad-hoc support packages — so you're never left on your own.
How is Stenvis BI different from hiring a freelancer?
+
A freelancer gives you one skill set. We bring a team — BI consultants, a data scientist, and a research lead — covering the full intelligence stack. We also bring a structured methodology, quality review process, and business context that individual freelancers rarely provide. You get a partner, not a vendor.
